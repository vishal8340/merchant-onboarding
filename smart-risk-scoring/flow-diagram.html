🔬 LIME Explainability ~500ms 10 FEATURES

Custom LIME — perturb 500 samples → weighted linear regression → feature importance for all 10 features
Includes: fraud_severity (ordinal 0-4 perturbation) + country_risk_score (clamped 0-100)
Output: feature, weight, displayValue, impact (CRITICAL/HIGH/MODERATE/LOW), reason (human-readable)
